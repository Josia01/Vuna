Our Founder

Dr. Wanjiku Nganga

BDS (Nairobi) · MFDS RCS (Edinburgh) · Lead Dentist & Implant Surgeon

Dr. Nganga founded Aurelle Dental in 2009 after returning from post-graduate training in restorative and implant dentistry at the University of Edinburgh. Her philosophy is simple: every patient deserves to leave the clinic feeling better than when they arrived — not just physically, but emotionally.

With over 3,000 implants placed and a reputation as one of Nairobi's most sought-after cosmetic dentists, Dr. Nganga leads by example — consulting patients personally and mentoring each member of the Aurelle team.

BDS, University of Nairobi (First Class Honours, 2004)
MFDS, Royal College of Surgeons Edinburgh (2007)
Diploma in Implant Dentistry, UCL London (2009)
Fellow, African Dental Association
Certified Invisalign Diamond Provider
