Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you accept insurance?
Yes — we accept all major Kenyan health insurance providers including Jubilee, AAR, Britam, CIC, and NHIF. Our front desk team will help verify your cover and process claims directly.
How long does whitening last?
Professional whitening results typically last 12–24 months depending on your diet and oral hygiene habits. We provide a take-home maintenance kit to help extend and top up your results.
Is root canal therapy painful?
Not at Aurelle Dental. Modern root canal therapy under proper local anaesthesia is no more uncomfortable than a standard filling. Most patients are surprised by how routine it feels.
How many aligners will I need?
The number varies by case complexity — typically between 12 and 48 sets worn over 6–18 months. During your free digital scan consultation, we'll give you a precise treatment timeline.
Can I get veneers if I have fillings?
In most cases, yes. Veneers are bonded to the front surface of teeth and don't interfere with existing fillings on the biting surface. Dr. Farah will assess your suitability at your consultation.
What's the implant healing time?
The implant itself integrates with the bone over 3–6 months (osseointegration). You'll have a temporary crown during this period so you won't be without a tooth. The final crown is fitted at the end of the healing phase.
