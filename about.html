Who We Are

More than a dental clinic — a place you actually want to visit

Aurelle Dental was founded by Dr. Wanjiku Nganga in 2009 with a single purpose: to prove that exceptional dental care and a calm, welcoming experience are not mutually exclusive. Too many people avoid the dentist out of fear, cost anxiety, or past trauma — and too many clinics do nothing to change that.

"We designed Aurelle to feel more like a spa than a surgery. The anxiety patients carry through the door should leave with them — replaced by confidence in their smile."

— Dr. Wanjiku Nganga, Founder

Today our team of six specialist clinicians treat over 800 patients a month from across Nairobi and beyond. Our Westlands clinic spans 650 sq m of purpose-built treatment space, fitted with the latest digital imaging, CAD/CAM crown milling, and laser therapy equipment.

What We Stand For

The principles behind every appointment

Patient-first always

Every decision we make — from our appointment scheduling to the materials we use — is guided by what's genuinely best for you, not what's easiest or most profitable for us.

Clinical excellence

We invest heavily in training, technology, and continuing education. Our clinicians hold post-graduate qualifications from leading institutions in the UK, USA, and South Africa.

Your time matters

We run on schedule. Our online booking system, SMS reminders, and streamlined check-in process ensure you're seen promptly — with absolutely no waiting room anxiety.

Transparent pricing

No hidden fees. No surprise invoices. Before any treatment begins, you receive a written cost breakdown so you can make informed decisions with complete peace of mind.

Community roots

We give back. Each quarter we run free dental outreach clinics in underserved Nairobi neighbourhoods, partnering with local schools to promote oral hygiene from an early age.

Sustainable practice

We've eliminated single-use plastics across all consumables, use LED lighting throughout, and partner with suppliers who share our commitment to environmental responsibility.

Our Journey

15 years of growing smiles

2009
The founding

Dr. Nganga opens Aurelle Dental in a modest two-chair clinic in Westlands with a vision to redefine the dental experience in Nairobi.

2012
Digital X-ray & first expansion

We invest in full digital radiography and expand to five chairs, welcoming our first specialist — Dr. Omondi joining as orthodontist.

2016
Implant centre launched

Installation of 3D cone-beam CT scanner and launch of our dedicated implant surgery suite — the first of its kind in Westlands.

2019
CAD/CAM same-day crowns

Addition of in-house CAD/CAM milling for same-day ceramic crowns and veneers — eliminating the two-week lab wait entirely.

2024
650 m² flagship clinic

Full relocation to our current purpose-built Sarit Centre premises — 10 treatment suites, a dedicated children's zone, and a patient lounge.
