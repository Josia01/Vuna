Common Questions

Booking FAQs

How quickly can I get an appointment?
For routine appointments, we typically have slots within 2–3 days. For emergencies, we keep daily slots reserved — call us directly for same-day urgent care.
Can I reschedule or cancel?
Yes — free to reschedule or cancel up to 2 hours before your appointment by calling us or replying to your SMS confirmation. No cancellation fee applies.
What should I bring to my appointment?
Your insurance card and member number (if applicable), any previous dental X-rays or records, and a list of current medications. Arrive 5 minutes early for your first visit.
Do you see children?
Absolutely. Dr. Grace Otieno is our paediatric specialist, and we have a dedicated children's zone designed to make young patients feel safe and excited about dental visits.
Is parking available?
Yes — Sarit Centre has ample covered parking. We validate parking for all patients — just bring your ticket to reception. The clinic is also a 5-minute walk from the Westlands matatu stage.
How do I pay for my treatment?
We accept M-Pesa, Visa, Mastercard, cash, and all major insurance providers. For larger treatment plans, we can discuss phased payment arrangements with our patient coordinator.
