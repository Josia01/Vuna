What We Offer

Comprehensive dental care under one roof

General Dentistry

Routine check-ups, professional cleanings, fillings and preventive care to keep your natural teeth healthy for life.

Teeth Whitening

Professional-grade in-chair whitening that lifts stains and brightens your smile by up to 8 shades in a single session.

Dental Implants

Permanent, natural-looking implants that restore your bite, speech and confidence with precision placement by our specialists.

Orthodontics

Clear aligners and traditional braces to gently straighten teeth and align your bite — for teens and adults alike.

Root Canal Therapy

Comfortable, same-visit root canal treatments using rotary instrumentation and local anaesthesia — completely painless.

Cosmetic Veneers

Ultra-thin porcelain veneers crafted to mask chips, gaps, and discolouration — giving you a Hollywood-quality smile.

Why Aurelle Dental

A practice built on trust, technology & tenderness

We believe going to the dentist shouldn't feel like a chore. Our clinic is designed to calm anxiety from the moment you walk through the door — with modern equipment, transparent pricing, and a team that truly listens. Every treatment plan is personalised, explained fully, and always puts your long-term oral health first.
